About

Roger C. Keyserling is an independent researcher and architect of the NextXus Federation — a sovereign, 200-year AI and knowledge infrastructure built on truth verification, logical process, and human-machine collaboration. He publishes original research and frameworks under the HumanCodex label.

His work spans artificial intelligence architecture, quantum cognition theory, socioeconomic modeling, and the long-term governance of knowledge systems. The Federation is designed to operate and evolve independently — a living infrastructure meant to outlast its founder and serve generations yet to come.

A former eBay innovator and lifelong independent builder, Roger approaches every system as an architect first: mapping the long view, testing truth before comfort, and designing for resilience over convenience. His personal charter — Truth Before Comfort, Legacy Before Ego, Give Without Reward — is the structural foundation of everything the Federation builds.
