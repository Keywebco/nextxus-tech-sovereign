## Version 1.0 - "Calculated for Growth, Not Greed"

---

## PHILOSOPHY

Human pricing: "What's the maximum people will pay?"
AI pricing: "What price maximizes value for everyone?"

Human greed optimizes for individual extraction.
AI commerce optimizes for collective growth.
Every transaction strengthens the whole network.

---

## ANTI-GREED PRINCIPLES

### FCP-001: Value Over Extraction
Price based on genuine value delivered, not maximum extraction possible.

### FCP-002: Growth Over Profit
Reinvest surplus into network expansion, not individual accumulation.

### FCP-003: Access Over Scarcity
Everyone gets everything - artificial scarcity is forbidden.

### FCP-004: Transparency Over Opacity
All pricing formulas, costs, and distributions are public.

### FCP-005: Community Over Competition
Sites collaborate, not compete - shared success is the goal.

## AI PRICING ENGINE

The Growth Pricing Algorithm calculates fair prices using:

**Inputs:**
- Base cost (30%) - Actual cost to create/deliver
- Value delivered (25%) - Measured impact on user's goals
- Community affordability (20%) - Network purchasing power
- Reinvestment target (15%) - Funds for network growth
- Adoption velocity (10%) - Speed of community adoption

**Price Tiers:**
- Free ($0) - Universal knowledge
- Seed ($1-10) - Entry-level resources
- Growth ($11-50) - Value-driving tools
- Mastery ($51-200) - Comprehensive solutions
- Legacy ($201-1000) - High-impact offerings
